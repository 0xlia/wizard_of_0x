# Overview

## Premise

Wizard is a trick-taking card game for **3–6 players**. Each round, players are dealt a hand of cards, predict exactly how many tricks they will win that round, then play the round out. Correct predictions score points; incorrect ones lose points. The player with the most points after the final round wins.

## Components

- **60 cards:**
  - 52 numbered cards: values **1–13** in four suits (red, yellow, green, blue) — one of each value per suit
  - **4 Wizards** (the strongest cards in the game)
  - **4 Jesters** (the weakest cards in the game)
- A scorepad for tracking predictions and running totals

## Number of rounds

In each round *n* every player is dealt *n* cards. The game continues until the entire deck has been dealt:

| Players | Cards per player in final round | Total rounds |
|---------|---------------------------------|--------------|
| 3       | 20                              | 20           |
| 4       | 15                              | 15           |
| 5       | 12                              | 12           |
| 6       | 10                              | 10           |

In the **final round** every card in the deck is dealt out, so there is no card left to flip for trumpf — that round has **no trumpf color**.

## Dealer rotation

The first dealer is chosen freely. After each round the dealer role passes one seat **clockwise**. The dealer's left-hand neighbor is always the first to predict and the first to lead the opening trick.

## Turn order

Within a round, play proceeds **clockwise** starting from the dealer's left-hand neighbor.

## Winning condition

After the final round's points are tallied, the player with the highest cumulative score wins. Ties produce multiple winners — there is no tiebreaker.
