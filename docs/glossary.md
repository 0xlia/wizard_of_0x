# Glossary

Terms used across the Wizard rule docs.

- **Dealer** — the player who deals cards and flips the trumpf card for the current round. Rotates clockwise after each round. If the trumpf flip is a Wizard, the dealer chooses the trumpf color.
- **Follow suit** — the obligation to play a card of the same color as the trick's lead card, if you hold any. See [tricks.md](tricks.md).
- **Hand** — the cards a player currently holds. In round *n*, each player's hand begins with *n* cards and shrinks by one per trick.
- **Jester** — one of the four colorless "weak" special cards. Loses to every numbered card and Wizard. Always legal to play. See [special-cards.md](special-cards.md).
- **Lead / Lead card** — the first card played into a trick. The player who plays it is the **leader** of that trick. The lead card's color (if it has one) sets the suit other players must follow.
- **Prediction** — the integer number of tricks a player commits to winning this round, announced before any card is played.
- **Round** — one cycle of deal → predict → play → score. The game consists of multiple rounds; see [overview.md](overview.md) for counts.
- **Scorepad of Truth** — the score sheet used to track predictions and running totals.
- **Stack** — the face-down pile of undealt cards. Only exists in non-final rounds; the top card of the stock is flipped face-up to determine trumpf.
- **Suit / Color** — one of red, yellow, green, blue.
- **Trick** — one card played by each player in clockwise order. Won by one player; that player leads the next trick. See [tricks.md](tricks.md).
- **Trumpf / Trumpf color** — the special color for the round, determined by the flipped card. Any numbered trumpf card beats any numbered card of another color. Wizards still beat trumpfs. Some rounds have no trump (Jester flipped, or final round).
- **Wizard** — one of the four colorless "strong" special cards. Beats every numbered card. The first Wizard played in a trick wins. Always legal to play. See [special-cards.md](special-cards.md).
